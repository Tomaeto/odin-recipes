Yummy Burger

Description

A healthy and yummy burger made with lean ground beef and tasty spices.

Yields 6 burger patties

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs extra-lean ground beef
  • 1 oz dry onion soup mix
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • 2 tsp hot pepper sauce
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 3/4 cup rolled oats

Steps

  1. Preheat an outdoor grill for medium high heat and lightly oil grate.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the beef, onion soup mix, egg, hotsauce and oats. Shape into 6 patties.
  3. Grill patties over medium high heat for 10 to 20 minutes, or to desired doneness.
