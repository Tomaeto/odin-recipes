Miso Soup

Description

A quick and simple miso soup, ready in just 15 minutes

Provides an authentic Japanese flavor with just 5 simple ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tsp dashi granules
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 8 oz silken tofu, diced
  • 2 green onions, sliced into 1/2 inch pieces

Steps

  1. Combine water and dashi granules in a medium saucepan over medium high heat. Bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce heat to medium and whisk in miso paste.
  3. Stir in tofu.
  4. Separate layers of green onions and add.
  5. Simmer gently for 3 minutes.
