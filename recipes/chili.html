Delicious Chili

Description

A delicious mild chili made with a blend of spices, beef, and vegetables

Hearty, healthy, and the perfect heat for mild taste buds

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 red jalapeno pepper, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2.5 lbs lean ground beef
  • 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 pinch garlic powder
  • 2 beef bouillon cubes
  • 28 oz crushed tomatoes
  • 14.5 oz fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 12 oz tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp chipotle pepper sauce
  • 2.5 tsp dried basil
  • 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 16 oz dark red kidney beans

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook and stir onion, peppers, and garlic until softened.
  2. Heat a large skilled over medium high heat. Cook and sir beef until browned.
  3. Add Worcestershire sauce and garlic powder. Crumble bouillon cubes over. Cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Stir beef mix into pepper mixture.
  5. Stir tomatoes and tomato paste into mixture. Season with chili powder, bown sugar, pepper sauce, basil, paprika, salt, and oregano.
  6. Bring to a boil and reduce heat to medium low. Cover and simmer for 90 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Mix kindey beans into mixture. Simmer for 30 minutes.
